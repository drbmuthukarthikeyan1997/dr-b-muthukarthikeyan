About Me

I am Dr. B. Muthukarthikeyan, an Assistant Professor of English, author, researcher, motivational speaker and social activist from Tamil Nadu.


My academic and literary interests include Indian Writing in English, mythology, comparative literature, gender studies, crime fiction, communication studies and English Language Teaching.
