About Me

I am Dr. B. Muthukarthikeyan, an Assistant Professor of English at P.S.R. Engineering College, Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu. I am an author, researcher, motivational speaker, academic mentor and social activist with academic interests spanning English Language Teaching, Indian Writing in English, Comparative Literature, Mythology, Gender Studies, Crime Fiction and Communication Studies.


My academic journey combines teaching, research, literary writing, student mentoring, professional communication training, youth development and community service.


As an academician, I focus on English language development, communication skills, professional English, employability skills, soft skills, academic writing and innovative teaching practices.
